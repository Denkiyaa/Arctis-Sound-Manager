The impulse responses in this directory (*.wav) and their descriptions
(info.csv) come from HeSuVi — Headphone Surround Virtualization for
Equalizer APO — by Matt Gore: https://sourceforge.net/projects/hesuvi/

Most of them are recordings of commercial surround virtualizers (Dolby,
DTS, Microsoft, Creative, Razer, Sennheiser, Nahimic, Waves, Out Of Your
Head…), made by HeSuVi and its community with those products switched on
(recorders are credited in info.csv). The names of those products are
trademarks of their respective owners; ASM is not affiliated with or
endorsed by any of them. The OpenAL Soft profiles (oal*) are rendered from
OpenAL Soft's own HRTF data sets.
